# coregold-usdt-vcc-onchain-to-card-wallet-workflow-guide

**Bridging Digital Assets & Everyday Payments: USDT (TRC20) to US Virtual Cards & Wallets**

Partnerships linking on-chain platforms with payment infrastructure—such as Mosta's European expansion with Crossmint and OpenPayd—highlight how builders and users increasingly look for smooth ways to connect digital funds with everyday payments.

For users who already hold or buy **USDT**, funding a **CoreGold** wallet over the **TRON (TRC20)** network provides a direct path to issuing US-issued virtual cards and binding them to **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**.

---

## Practical Knowledge: 4 Steps to Smooth USDT-to-Wallet Usage

### 1. Always Select TRON (TRC20) When Funding USDT
Whether transferring from a crypto wallet or buying USDT first on an exchange like OKX or Binance, choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network and verify the deposit address, network, and amount before confirming. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### 2. Choose the Right US-Issued Card BIN Category
CoreGold provides **US-issued card BINs** across AI tools, Apple Pay / Google Pay, Google Play / Apple Store, and overseas e-commerce, ads, and cloud services—pick the category matching your target checkout.

### 3. Keep Billing Addresses Consistent
Whether using **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or manual entry (Name, State, City, Street Address, ZIP Code), ensure your checkout billing address matches the card's recorded billing information.

### 4. Bind to Apple Pay or Google Pay in Four Quick Steps
All CoreGold card BINs support **Apple Pay** and **Google Pay**. Enter card details manually in your wallet app, choose **email verification**, retrieve the code under **Add to Digital Wallet** on the CoreGold card details page, and enter it back into your wallet.

> **Card Tip**: Keep full card numbers, CVVs, and verification codes private, and freeze idle cards inside **Card Management** whenever you aren't actively using them.

## Simple Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

### Step 1: Register on CoreGold
Visit [https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b), register using your regular email address, and log in to the user dashboard.

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. In the left menu, click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)**.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, verify the address, network, and amount, and confirm the transfer. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Details
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription) or **Team Plan**.
2. Pick the matching US-issued card BIN category and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Check **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or uncheck it to enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during merchant checkout.

### Step 4: View Card & Bind Apple Pay / Google Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze controls.
2. To link **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**:
   - Open **Apple Wallet** on iPhone or **Google Wallet** on Android, select **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Choose **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into your mobile wallet to complete binding.
